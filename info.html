Travel Information

Essential details to prepare for your African safari adventure

Planning Your Safari

Everything you need to know before embarking on your African safari journey.

Before You Go

Visa Requirements

Most African countries require visas for international visitors. Requirements vary by country:

  • Kenya: eVisa available online
  • Tanzania: Visa on arrival for most nationalities
  • South Africa: Visa-free for many countries (check requirements)
  • Botswana: Visa-free for most visitors

Always check the latest requirements with the embassy 2-3 months before travel.

Health & Vaccinations

Health precautions are essential for African travel:

  • Yellow Fever vaccination certificate required for many countries
  • Malaria prophylaxis recommended for most safari destinations
  • Consult your doctor 6-8 weeks before travel
  • Pack a comprehensive medical kit including anti-malarials
What to Pack

Essential packing list for your safari:

  • Neutral-colored clothing (khaki, beige, olive green)
  • Lightweight, breathable fabrics
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses
  • High SPF sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Binoculars and camera equipment
  • Light jacket for cool mornings and evenings

During Your Safari

Safari Etiquette

Important guidelines for wildlife viewing:

  • Always remain seated in safari vehicles
  • Keep voices low when wildlife is nearby
  • Never attempt to feed or touch animals
  • Follow your guide's instructions at all times
  • Respect minimum distance requirements
Weather & Seasons

Understanding African seasons:

  • Dry Season (June-October): Best for wildlife viewing as animals gather at water sources
  • Green Season (November-May): Lush landscapes, fewer tourists, bird-watching paradise
  • Migration Season (July-October): Witness the Great Migration in East Africa
  • Temperatures vary from 15°C (59°F) at night to 35°C (95°F) during the day
Photography Tips

Capturing the perfect safari shots:

  • Bring a telephoto lens (200-400mm recommended)
  • Use bean bags or window mounts for stability
  • Shoot during golden hours (early morning and late afternoon)
Important Travel Note

Travel insurance is mandatory for all our safari packages. Ensure your policy covers medical evacuation, trip cancellation, and adventure activities. Check visa requirements for all countries you'll visit or transit through, as regulations change frequently.
