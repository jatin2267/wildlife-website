Wildlife of Africa

Discover the magnificent creatures that roam the African savannahs

Featured Species

Africa is home to some of the world's most iconic wildlife. Here are a few you might encounter on your safari adventure.

Monitor Lizard

Stealthy and resilient, the monitor lizard slinks through the undergrowth and riverbanks, its forked tongue flicking as it scans for prey with cold-blooded precision.

Black Rhinoceros (rare)

Rare and powerful, the black rhinoceros roams the bush with a fierce presence, its hooked lip and sharp senses suited for survival in the wild heart of the Mara.

Crowned Crane

Graceful and regal, the crowned crane struts through wetlands and grasslands, its golden crest shimmering in the sun a true jewel of the Mara skies.

African Lion

The apex predator of the savannah, lions are frequently spotted in prides throughout the reserve.

African Elephant

Majestic herds of elephants roam the grasslands and gather near watering holes.

Cheetah

Witness the world's fastest land animal in action during thrilling hunts.

African Caracal

Sleek and elusive, the caracal is a solitary hunter known for its powerful leaps and signature tufted ears, silently prowling the savannah in search of prey.

Martial Eagle

With piercing eyes and a wingspan over two meters, the martial eagle soars high above the plains, striking prey with unmatched precision and power.

Wildebeest

Famous for their epic migrations, wildebeests thunder across the plains in massive herds, braving rivers and predators in a timeless struggle for survival.
